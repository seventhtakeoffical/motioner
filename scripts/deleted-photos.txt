That embarrassing photo you deleted years ago is very likely still sitting on your phone right now. Because hitting delete never actually erased it. It just throws away the map that leads to it. Think of your storage like a giant library. Every photo is a book on a shelf. Deleting does not burn the book. It just rips out the one card in the catalog, the card that told your phone which shelf to check. And that little card is called a pointer. The book, your photo, is still sitting right there. Your phone just forgot where it put it, and marked that shelf as free. It sits there until your phone drops a new file, right on top of it. So deleted doesn't really mean gone, it just means forgotten.
